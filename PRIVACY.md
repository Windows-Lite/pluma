# Pluma privacy policy

Pluma is a web browser that runs on your computer. It has no servers and collects nothing about you.

- Your bookmarks, history, passwords, cookies and settings stay on your computer.
- Sync is off unless you turn it on. When you do, Pluma encrypts a backup with a password only you know and stores it
  in the destination you choose: a folder, or your own Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox.
- With Google Drive, Pluma asks only for `drive.appdata`: a hidden folder for Pluma's own backups. It cannot see any
  other file in your Drive. The sign-in token stays on your computer, protected with your Windows account.
- Nobody, including us, can read the backup without your password.
- Translation sends the text of the page you ask to translate to Google Translate. Nothing is translated unless you
  ask.
- Updates are checked against this repository's GitHub releases.

Questions: open an issue in https://github.com/Windows-Lite/pluma.
